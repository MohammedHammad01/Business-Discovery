# Northwind Facilities — Purchase Request Procedure (v4, revised Jan 2026)

## 1. Purpose

This document describes how site teams request goods and services, and how those
requests are approved before an order is placed.

## 2. Raising a request

2.1 The site supervisor emails the Operations Manager with a description of the
goods or services required, the site name, and — where available — a supplier
quote.

2.2 Requests received by telephone or messaging application must be confirmed by
email by the end of the working day.

2.3 The Operations Manager records the request in the Purchase Request
spreadsheet held on the shared drive (`\\NWFS01\Ops\PurchaseRequests.xlsx`) and
allocates the next sequential reference number.

## 3. Approval limits

| Value (excl. VAT) | Approver                    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| Up to £500        | Operations Manager          |
| £501 – £5,000     | Finance Manager             |
| Over £5,000       | Operations Director         |
| Any value, emergency works | Finance Manager (retrospective approval permitted within 48 hours) |

## 4. Approval

4.1 The Operations Manager forwards the request by email to the relevant
approver.

4.2 The approver replies to the email indicating approval or rejection. Reasons
for rejection should be stated.

4.3 The Operations Manager updates the status column in the spreadsheet.

## 5. Ordering

5.1 Once approved, the site supervisor is informed and may place the order with
the supplier.

5.2 Invoices are matched against the spreadsheet reference by Finance and keyed
into Sage.

## 6. Records

6.1 The spreadsheet is the record of requests and their outcome.

6.2 Approval evidence is held in the approver's mailbox.

6.3 Records are retained for six years.

## 7. Known issues (added at the January review)

- Status in the spreadsheet is frequently out of date.
- Approval evidence is difficult to retrieve for audit.
- No agreed turnaround time is defined in this procedure.
